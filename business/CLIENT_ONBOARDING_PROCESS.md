## 3. Step-by-Step Onboarding Process

### Step 1: Initial Inquiry & Discovery
- **Discovery Call / Meeting:** B2C Solution meets with the Client to discuss project goals, technical scope, timeline, and budget expectations.
- **Feasibility Assessment:** B2C Solution evaluates internal technical capacity and project alignment.

### Step 2: Proposal & Quotation
- **Scope Definition:** B2C Solution drafts a detailed quotation using `business/QUOTATION_TEMPLATE.md`.
- **Review & Agreement:** The proposal outlines key deliverables, timeline estimates, payment terms, and project milestones.

### Step 3: Legal & Confidentiality Framework
Before work or sensitive technical discussions begin:
- **NDA / Confidentiality:** Execution of `legal/NDA_CONFIDENTIALITY_AGREEMENT.md` to protect Client proprietary information.
- **Client Service Agreement:** Execution of `legal/CLIENT_SERVICE_AGREEMENT.md` covering service scope, IP rights (`legal/INTELLECTUAL_PROPERTY_POLICY.md`), liability, and warranty terms.

### Step 4: Requirement Gathering & Asset Collection
- **Asset Intake:** Client provides necessary assets (brand guidelines, logos, content, API credentials, third-party integrations).
- **Communication Channel Setup:** Establish official project communication channels (Email, Slack, or designated messaging group).
- **Key Contacts Identified:**
  - **B2C Solution Leads:** Om Harde (Founder), Purvesh Bhadale (Co-Founder)
  - **Client Lead:** Designated Client Representative

### Step 5: Deposit & Invoicing
- **Advance Invoice:** B2C Solution issues an advance invoice according to `business/INVOICE_TERMS.md` (typically 40% deposit).
- **Payment Settlement:** Work is scheduled upon receipt of advance payment.

### Step 6: Official Kickoff & Sprint Planning
- **Kickoff Meeting:** Alignment on project roadmap, milestone delivery dates, and feedback loops.
- **Delivery Initiation:** Transition to the project execution stage as defined in `business/PROJECT_DELIVERY_PROCESS.md`.
