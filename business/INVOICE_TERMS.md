## 3. Invoicing Structure and Payment Schedule

Unless explicitly specified otherwise in a signed `legal/CLIENT_SERVICE_AGREEMENT.md` or `business/QUOTATION_TEMPLATE.md`, B2C Solution operates on the following milestone invoicing structure:

1. **Advance Deposit (40%):** Invoiced upon project acceptance. Work begins only after the initial advance payment is received.
2. **Milestone Progress Payment (40%):** Invoiced upon delivery and demonstration of the project's primary staging deliverable or beta milestone.
3. **Final Balance (20%):** Invoiced upon completion of testing and prior to final production deployment, source code transfer, or domain launch.

---

## 4. Payment Due Dates

- **Standard Invoices:** Payment is due within **7 business days** from the invoice issue date unless otherwise agreed in writing.
- **Advance Invoices:** Payment is due immediately upon receipt prior to project commencement.
- **Production Delivery:** Source code deployment and final deliverable transfer are conditional upon full settlement of all outstanding invoices.

---

## 5. Accepted Payment Methods

B2C Solution accepts payments through approved digital channels, including:

- **Bank Wire Transfer / NEFT / RTGS / IMPS** (India);
- **UPI (Unified Payments Interface)** (India);
- **International Wire Transfer / SWIFT** (International Clients);
- **Approved Payment Gateway Links** (e.g., Stripe, Razorpay, or equivalent payment links provided on official invoices).

Specific banking details and payment links are provided on official B2C Solution invoices.

---

## 6. Currency and Transaction Fees

- **Local Transactions (India):** Invoiced in Indian Rupees (INR).
- **International Transactions:** Invoiced in United States Dollars (USD) or other mutually agreed foreign currency.
- **Transaction Charges:** The Client is responsible for any bank transfer fees, intermediary banking charges, currency conversion fees, or payment gateway processing fees to ensure B2C Solution receives the full net invoiced amount.

---

## 7. Late Payments and Project Delays

- **Project Suspension:** If an invoice remains unpaid past the due date, B2C Solution reserves the right to temporarily pause project work until payment is settled.
- **Timeline Adjustments:** Any delay in client payment will result in an equivalent or extended adjustment to the project delivery schedule.
- **Late Fees:** B2C Solution reserves the right to charge reasonable interest on severely overdue invoices where permitted by applicable law and contract.

---

## 8. Third-Party Costs

Third-party costs (such as domain registration, cloud hosting, premium API subscriptions, or paid software licenses) incurred by B2C Solution on behalf of the Client are non-refundable and invoiced separately or included as designated pass-through items.

---

## 9. Refunds and Billing Disputes

- **Refund Policy:** All refunds and cancellations are handled strictly according to `legal/REFUND_AND_CANCELLATION_POLICY.md`.
- **Billing Disputes:** If a Client believes an invoice contains an error, the Client must notify B2C Solution in writing at `b2csolution2436@gmail.com` within **5 business days** of invoice receipt. Non-disputed portions of the invoice remain payable by the due date.
