## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Conflict of Interest Policy is to protect the integrity, legal standing, business reputation, and decision-making processes of **B2C Solution**.

As an early-stage startup, B2C Solution requires all founders, team members, contractors, and contributors to act in the best interests of the organization and to disclose actual, potential, or perceived conflicts of interest.

---

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- Founders, co-founders, and leadership;
- Team members, employees, and contractors;
- Technical contributors and designers;
- Strategic advisors or partners representing B2C Solution.

---

## 3. Definition of Conflict of Interest

A **Conflict of Interest** arises when an individual's personal, financial, family, or external professional interests interfere—or appear to interfere—with their duty, loyalty, or objective judgment toward B2C Solution.

Conflicts may be:

- **Actual:** A current situation where personal interest directly conflicts with B2C Solution duties.
- **Potential:** A situation that may develop into an actual conflict in the future.
- **Perceived:** A situation where a reasonable third party would perceive a conflict, even if no improper motive exists.

## 5. Common Examples of Conflicts

Examples of potential conflicts of interest include:

- **Outside Business Activities:** Engaging in external software development or consulting that competes directly with B2C Solution services or targets B2C Solution clients.
- **Personal Financial Interests:** Recommending a vendor, software tool, or service provider in which the individual or an immediate family member has a financial or ownership interest without disclosure.
- **Client & Lead Diversion:** Diverting potential business leads, client opportunities, or contracts from B2C Solution to a personal entity or third party.
- **Misuse of Proprietary Information:** Using B2C Solution source code, client lists, project architectures, or confidential trade secrets for personal gain or external projects without written approval.
- **Gifts and Hospitality:** Accepting substantial gifts, discounts, or favors from suppliers, clients, or partners that could influence professional judgment.

---

## 6. Disclosure Obligations and Procedure

1. **Mandatory Disclosure:** Individuals must promptly disclose any actual, potential, or perceived conflict of interest to the Founder or Co-Founder in writing at `b2csolution2436@gmail.com`.
2. **Timing:** Disclosures should be made as soon as the conflict becomes known, or prior to entering into a new external engagement or transaction.
3. **Review & Determination:** Leadership will review the disclosure to determine whether a material conflict exists and what mitigation steps are required.

---

## 7. Conflict Resolution and Mitigation

Depending on the nature of the conflict, B2C Solution leadership may require:

- **Recusal:** Abstaining from decision-making or voting regarding the affected vendor, contract, or client.
- **Adjustment of Responsibilities:** Reassigning specific project tasks to avoid bias or misuse of confidential information.
- **Divestment or Refusal:** Discontinuing the external activity or declining the gift/proposal.
- **Permitted Engagement:** Approving the activity with explicit conditions or boundaries documented in writing.
